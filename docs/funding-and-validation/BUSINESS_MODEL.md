# Business Model & Go-to-Market (hypotheses)

These are **hypotheses to validate in pilots**, not proven facts. Pricing and packaging must follow demonstrated workflow value and the evidence/regulatory posture in `VALIDATION_AND_REGULATORY.md`.

## Buyer & value

| Segment | Pain today | Value NeuLens targets | Likely buyer |
|---|---|---|---|
| Memory / geriatric clinics | Follow-up is episodic, documentation-heavy, hard to compare over time | Consistent longitudinal screen + evidence-linked review record; remote/browser follow-up between visits | Clinic/practice lead, medical director |
| Primary care (older adults) | Cognitive screening is time-consuming, inconsistently done/documented | Fast guided screen that fits a visit and produces a reviewable record to support reassure/repeat/refer | PC group ops / quality lead |
| Pharma/CRO (later) | Trial pre-screening and longitudinal cognitive endpoints are costly | Low-burden, repeatable digital assessments (requires validation & fit-for-purpose evidence) | Study/innovation lead |

**Value metric:** completed, reviewable (usable-quality, comparable) screening sessions that save clinician time or improve follow-up decisions — not raw tests started.

## Packaging & pricing (to test)

- **Clinic subscription (SaaS):** tiered by size/volume (e.g. per clinician seat or per-site monthly) including a pool of screens, review/reporting, and exports. Aligns with workflow adoption; predictable for clinics.
- **Per-completed-screen:** usage-based (billed on completed, reviewable sessions) — simpler to start in pilots, maps to value delivered.
- **Paid pilot → annual contract:** time-boxed paid pilot with explicit success criteria and a pre-agreed conversion price/term. Avoid indefinite free pilots.
- **Enterprise/health-system:** site license + implementation/support; requires integrations, security assurance, and stronger evidence.

*Benchmarks to gather in pilots:* staff minutes saved per screen/review, current screening/reimbursement context, and what the site pays today for comparable workflow time or tools. Do not guess reimbursement applicability — confirm with billing/compliance experts per jurisdiction and evidence.

## Go-to-market (wedge-first)

1. **Land:** design-partner memory/geriatric clinics and PC groups via clinical champions; prove the longitudinal follow-up workflow (narrow, high-pain). Win on ease of deployment (browser, no install) and evidence-linked review.
2. **Prove & convert:** convert pilots to paid on documented workflow value; produce case-based evidence and clinician references.
3. **Expand:** broaden within accounts (more clinicians/patients), then to adjacent segments (primary-care networks, senior living with clinician oversight) as evidence and integrations mature.
4. **Deepen moat:** validated within-person change metrics, longitudinal dataset (governed/consented), EHR/workflow integrations, and site-level outcomes data.

## Unit-economics drivers (to measure early)

- **Activation:** sites trained and running real sessions; time-to-first-reviewed-session.
- **Engagement/retention:** screens per patient over time (longitudinal value), clinician weekly active reviewers, site renewal.
- **Delivery cost:** cloud/compute per session (edge-heavy capture helps), support minutes per site/session, onboarding cost.
- **Gross margin path:** software margins improve as support/onboarding are standardized and edge processing keeps variable compute low; validate with real usage.

## What we will not do (yet)

- No consumer self-diagnosis product or DTC diagnostic claims.
- No outcome/reimbursement claims without jurisdiction-specific evidence and compliance review.
- No broad “platform for everything” sell before the wedge is repeatably adopted and paid.
