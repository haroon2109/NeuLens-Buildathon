# NeuLens Pitch — Startup Buildathon India 2026

**Lakshmi is 66 and lives in Coimbatore.** Her daughter noticed she occasionally loses her train of thought during morning conversations, but Lakshmi laughs it off: *"வயதானால் மறதி சகஜம், அம்மாக்கு ஒண்ணுமில்ல"* ("Forgetting is normal when getting older, nothing is wrong with me"). In India, this story plays out in tens of millions of homes. Nobody visits a ₹15,000 specialist until symptoms become catastrophic.

**Lakshmi takes the 5-minute NeuLens screening on her daughter’s tablet.**
- Spoken instructions in Tamil guide her through five quick activities.
- She tracks a moving orb with her eyes, matches familiar pictures of a Diya, Mango, Peacock, and Lotus, repeats a simple sentence about the banyan tree and village pond, and completes a guided trail-making task.
- Zero complex clinical jargon. Zero fear. Zero expensive equipment.

**Six months later, Lakshmi screens again.**
Because language, education years, task version, and device conditions match her earlier sessions, NeuLens compares her performance not against a generic Western textbook average, but against **her own personal baseline anchor**.
- The system detects a subtle, reliable decline in ocular fixation hold stability and verbal fluency cadence ($\Delta \le -5.0$ Reliable Change Index).
- It does **not** say *"You have Alzheimer's."* NeuLens strictly refuses to make diagnostic claims.
- Instead, it generates a structured, evidence-backed clinician dossier powered by **Google Gemini 2.5 Flash** for her local doctor: *“Is this an early signal of mild cognitive impairment worth investigating, or was she simply fatigued and sleep-deprived?”*

That question is the product. **NeuLens turns everyday smartphones into accessible, culturally adapted early cognitive monitoring clinics across India.**

## One Sentence

**NeuLens is an accessible, multimodal cognitive screening and longitudinal brain-health monitoring platform for India, powered on-device by Google MediaPipe and in the cloud by Google Gemini 2.5 Flash.**

---

## The 5 Pillars of Our Buildathon Solution

1. **Indian Languages & Cultural Stimuli**: Native support for English, Tamil (`ta`), and Hindi (`hi`), featuring familiar Indian everyday objects (Diya, Mango, Peacock, Lotus) rather than abstract Western test figures.
2. **Education-Aware & Literacy-Inclusive**: Integrated vernacular audio readouts and pictogram-driven interactions ensure fair assessment across all formal education levels.
3. **Personal Longitudinal Baseline**: Within-person change detection ($\Delta \le -5.0$ RCI) eliminates population bias and catches subtle changes months or years before traditional clinical tests.
4. **On-Device Edge Privacy**: 100% of facial mesh and ocular iris tracking runs in-browser via **Google MediaPipe** WebAssembly/WebGL. Zero raw photos or video frames leave the patient's device, ensuring compliance with India's DPDP Act 2023.
5. **Clinician Decision Support with Google AI**: Deterministic scoring paired with **Google Gemini 2.5 Flash** synthesizes clinical summaries for neurologist review and approval.
