# Limitations

This is a hackathon prototype, not a validated clinical product.

- The screening activities are designed as guided observations, not as validated cognitive instruments.
- Scoring outputs are exploratory and do not represent a diagnosis, disease probability, or validated reliable-change statistic.
- The explanation layer is constrained, but any drafted text is auxiliary and must be reviewed by a clinician before it is shared.
- Webcam and microphone input are limited by ordinary hardware. Tracking and recording quality are reported alongside the measurements for that reason.
- If the database is unreachable, persistence may fall back to a development mode. That is a fallback posture, not a verified production persistence story.
- Professional-account verification, care workflows, and some portal features are only partially integrated into the current demo spine.
- This project is not a substitute for clinical judgment, in-person assessment, or established screening tools.
